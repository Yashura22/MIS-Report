# MIS & Business Workflow Automation System

A custom **MIS and business workflow automation system** designed to digitize and automate day-to-day operational processes, reporting, and data management.

The system is built around **Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, providing a centralized platform for managing workflows, generating MIS reports, tracking inventory, and controlling user access.

## Key Features

* **FMS Workflow Management** — Track client-wise workflows, activities, status, and responses.
* **Automated MIS Reporting** — Generate date-wise and process-wise MIS reports from operational data.
* **Inventory Management (IMS)** — Track item/lot inward and outward movements with quantity calculations.
* **Role-Based Access Control** — Different permissions for management, sales, accounts, executives, and other users.
* **Interactive Dashboard** — Operational KPIs, inventory information, workflow status, and summary widgets.
* **Audit Logs** — Track important user activities and system changes.
* **Search & Filtering** — Quickly find clients, items, workflows, and operational records.
* **Mobile-Friendly Interface** — Designed for practical use across desktop and mobile devices.
* **Centralized Data Management** — Reduces manual reporting and repetitive spreadsheet operations.
* **Automated Workflow Processing** — Minimizes manual data entry and improves consistency across business processes.

## Purpose

The primary objective of this project is to transform a spreadsheet-based operational workflow into a **structured business application**, reducing manual MIS work and providing users with real-time access to operational information.

The system demonstrates practical implementation of **business process automation, MIS development, workflow management, RBAC, dashboard development, inventory management, and data-driven reporting**.
